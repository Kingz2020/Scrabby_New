Joker Economy

Scrabby gives you and the AI the same six tiles each round, for four rounds; whoever builds the better word wins the round and their word goes on the board. The proposal: one joker per game, sitting in the corner, that becomes any letter for zero points and costs you a flat penalty.

The question this answers: does "hold it or spend it" feel like a real decision across four rounds, is −12 the right price, and should the joker count toward the 50-point six-tile bonus?

Rules you can change

"Yes" reproduces what the code does today: the bonus fires when total tiles placed equals six, so a five-tile word plus the joker collects it, and a six-tile word plus the joker loses it.

What happens over many games

Four rounds is a small sample, and one game tells you very little about whether −12 is the right number. This plays a thousand games under each cost rule with a player who always spends the joker on the single best opportunity they see, and reports how often that actually paid off.

Delete your account and data

Last updated: 23 September 2026

You can delete your Scrabby account yourself, from inside the game. It takes a few seconds and does not need my help.

In the game

  1. Open Scrabby and go to the sign-in card, where your name is shown.
  2. Tap Delete my account, under the Log out button.
  3. Type your password to confirm, and tap Delete my account again.

Your account is deleted immediately. You are signed out and cannot sign in with that email address again. You can always make a new account later.

By email

If you cannot get into the game - a lost password, a lost phone - write to mystakingz@gmail.com from the email address of the account, with the subject "Delete my Scrabby account". I will delete it and confirm, usually within a few days and at most within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account, with your email address and password.
  • Your player profile and player name.
  • Your list of games and invitations.
  • The notification token for your phone.

What stays, and why

Games you have finished remain in the game histories of the people you played against, because those are their records as much as yours. Your name in them is replaced with "Former player", and nothing in them identifies you: no email address, no profile, nothing that can be traced back to you.

Anything the app kept on your phone alone - the sound setting, your progress statistics - disappears when you uninstall it.

SCRABBY

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 September 2026

Scrabby is a word game for Android, made by Kingsley Obeng ("Kingz"), a private individual. This policy explains what the game stores about you, why, and how to get rid of it.

The short version: Scrabby stores what it needs to run your games and nothing else. There are no adverts, no analytics, no tracking, and nothing is ever sold or handed to anyone for marketing.

What is stored

  • Your email address and password, so you can sign in and keep your games. Passwords are handled by Google Firebase Authentication, which stores them encrypted. I never see them.
  • Your player name, which is shown to the people you play against.
  • Your games: the letters dealt, the words played, the scores, who played whom, and when. This is what a match is.
  • A notification token for your phone, so the game can tell you when it is your move. It identifies the app on your device, not you personally.

Some things stay on your phone only and are never sent anywhere: your sound setting, whether you have seen the tutorial, and your progress statistics. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What is not stored

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No analytics or usage tracking.
  • No location, contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera.
  • No payment details. The game is free and sells nothing.

Who can see it

People you play with see your player name, the words you play and your scores in your shared games. They never see your email address.

The data is held on Google Firebase servers in the European Union (Belgium), which stores it on my behalf. Nobody else has access, and nothing is sold, rented or shared for advertising. I would only hand anything over if the law required it.

Why it is allowed (GDPR)

Your account and your games are stored to provide the game you asked to play - in legal terms, to perform the agreement between us. Notifications are sent for the same reason, and you can switch them off in your phone's settings at any time.

How long it is kept

Your account and games are kept until you delete your account. After that, your account, your profile and your notification token are removed. Finished games remain in your opponents' own game histories, because those are their records too, and your name in them is replaced with "Former player".

Deleting your account

In the game: open the sign-in card, tap Delete my account, and type your password to confirm. Your account is deleted straight away.

There is a fuller description, including how to ask me to do it for you, on the account deletion page.

Your rights

You can ask for a copy of what is stored about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted. Write to the address below and I will answer within 30 days. If you are in the EU or the UK and you are not happy with my answer, you can complain to your national data protection authority.

Children

Scrabby is not aimed at children under 13, and I do not knowingly keep data about them. If a child of yours has made an account, write to me and I will delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version appears on this page with a new date at the top.

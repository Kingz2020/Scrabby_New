A word duel for Android

Same six letters. Best word wins.

How it works

  • You and your opponent get the same six letters, so nobody wins on luck.
  • Build the best word you can on a small board, with bonus squares scattered fresh every round.
  • Four rounds, and the higher score takes each one.
  • Play against a friend at your own pace, or on your own against the computer.

Free, and quiet about you

No adverts, no tracking, no in-app purchases. Scrabby stores what it needs to run your games and nothing else.
